Practical action

Protect the whole water system

Water security depends on rooftops, slopes, drains, treatment plants, groundwater recharge and riverbanks—not only the river channel.

01 / Capture

Harvest rainwater

Use suitable rooftop harvesting and recharge systems after checking local conditions and maintenance needs.

02 / Recharge

Restore springsheds

Protect recharge zones, soil and vegetation; support community-led watershed planning.

03 / Treat

Keep sewage out

Maintain sewerage and treatment plants, and monitor treated effluent and receiving waters.

04 / Plan

Build with water in mind

Protect natural drains, wetlands, floodplains and permeable ground during urban development.

05 / Measure

Publish open data

Show sampling date, location, parameter, units, method and agency so results can be compared fairly.

06 / Participate

Support local action

Report leaks or dumping to responsible agencies and check whether funded works remain functional.

Important data limitations

  • Census: India has no completed 2026 Census count available for this project. Use Census 2011 as the latest completed census baseline, or clearly label newer figures as projections/estimates from a named source. The 2001–2011 comparison is historical.
  • Ganga 2025: the government update says Uttarakhand's Ganga main-stem stretch was no longer a Polluted River Stretch and met the stated bathing criteria for key parameters. This does not prove every tributary, drain, spring or drinking-water source is safe.
  • Beneficiaries: expected project beneficiaries are not the same as actual verified outcomes.
  • Map: the clickable map is a static illustration, not a GIS boundary dataset; pins are reference points and no live sensor data is displayed.
